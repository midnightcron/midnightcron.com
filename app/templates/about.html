about

Robin Worreby. Based in Zürich. Data scientist by background, currently AI Implementation Expert at aiaibot, building automation and process improvement with generative AI (RAG, agents, bots). Off-hours I build small data tools and host them on a Raspberry Pi 4 sitting on my desk.

The default stack for everything here is Python: FastAPI plus Jinja, HTMX for the interactive bits, Pico.css for styling, SQLite or TimescaleDB depending on the data shape. Each service runs in Docker Compose behind Caddy and a Cloudflare Tunnel. Boring, fast, nothing exotic, runs on a 35-watt computer.

Project code lives at github.com/midnightcron. Personal account: github.com/rworreby. Reach me at robin@worreby.ch.
